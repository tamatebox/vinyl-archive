History

Everything the buffer still holds, plus every recording you kept. Buffered entries are reclaimed as the ring fills up; kept ones stay until you delete them. Keep works here too, for as long as the audio is still in the buffer. Pick a day to see just that day — days the buffer no longer reaches are greyed out, so the calendar also shows how far back it goes.

Playback

Still on disk, and given up first when the volume runs low — before any buffered audio, and before any recording you have not deleted. Until then nothing here costs you anything.
